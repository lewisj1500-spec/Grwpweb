Our vision
—Why We're Building This

We got tired of it. The hours lost to a feed. The group chat where every plan goes to die. Watching everyone else's life instead of living our own.

We don't think the only options are to swallow the worst of social media, or quit and lose touch. So we're building the third way — the good of being connected, without the noise. An app whose whole job is to get you off it, and back out there.

Stop watching everyone else's life. Go live yours.

— The Grwp team

—How It Works

Three taps from
feed to front door.

1

Swipe, just for you

Browse the places and plans near you and tap what catches your eye. It's completely private — nobody sees your picks, so there's nothing to perform.

2

The group quietly agrees

When you and your people land on the same thing, Grwp surfaces it as a plan. No polls, no “where do you want to go?”, no one stuck organising.

3

Show up and live it

Coffee, a run, dinner, a gig — whatever you matched on. Go, be there, and keep the memories somewhere that's yours, not a public feed.

—One App, Three Ways To Connect

However you
want to connect

1
Friends

The people you already know

You know how everyone says “we should do something” and then nothing happens? On Grwp, when you and your mates land on the same idea, you actually see it — so the plan's already half-made before anyone's had to be the organiser.

2
Community

The groups you belong to

Whether you're into running, reading, or just want to make some friends — Grwp gives everyone somewhere to belong.

3
Doorstep

The places right near you

There's good stuff ten minutes from your door you've never even heard of. Grwp shows you the cafés, the walks, the little gigs — the bits of your own city that are easy to miss until someone points them out.
